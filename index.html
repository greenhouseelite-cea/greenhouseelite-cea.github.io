ABOUT GREENHOUSE ELITE

Engineering-Based Controlled Environment Agriculture

GREENHOUSE ELITE is a scientific and engineering platform dedicated to greenhouse engineering and Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA).

The platform focuses on the integration of environmental control, plant physiology, lighting, irrigation, fertigation, energy management, sensing, automation and data-driven decision making.

Core Approach

  • Plant-centered engineering
  • Evidence-based design
  • Energy-efficient systems
  • Precision environmental control
  • Sensor-based monitoring
  • Smart and automated operation
