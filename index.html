ABOUT GREENHOUSE ELITE

Engineering-Based Controlled Environment Agriculture

GREENHOUSE ELITE is a scientific and engineering platform dedicated to greenhouse engineering and Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA).

The platform focuses on the integration of environmental control, plant physiology, lighting, irrigation, fertigation, energy management, sensing, automation and data-driven decision making.

Core Approach

  • Plant-centered engineering
  • Evidence-based design
  • Energy-efficient systems
  • Precision environmental control
  • Sensor-based monitoring
  • Smart and automated operation

RESEARCH

Research & Scientific Development

Research and engineering activities focused on the design, analysis and optimization of controlled production systems.

Greenhouse Engineering

Greenhouse climate, ventilation, heating, cooling, environmental control, energy management and engineering optimization of production conditions.

Controlled Environment Agriculture

Integrated control of temperature, humidity, VPD, CO₂, light and airflow to create precise and stable plant-growth environments.

Hydroponics & Soilless Systems

Irrigation, fertigation, nutrient solution management, water quality, root-zone conditions and recirculating soilless production systems.

Plant Factory & PFAL

Multi-layer crop production under fully controlled environmental conditions, with integrated lighting, HVAC, airflow and resource management.

Lighting & Photobiology

PPFD, DLI, spectrum, photoperiod, photon efficacy and dynamic lighting strategies for plant growth, quality and energy efficiency.

Smart Agriculture & Automation

Sensors, IoT, data acquisition, intelligent control, modeling, digital twins and data-driven decision making for controlled production.
